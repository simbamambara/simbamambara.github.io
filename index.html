About

I am an applied economist, data scientist, and AI researcher working at the intersection of economics, law, public policy, and technology.

My work focuses on using data and AI to solve practical problems in African institutions. I work across trade policy, taxation, consumer welfare, education, legal research, and decision-support systems.

I am especially interested in how machine learning, information retrieval, and structured knowledge systems can make complex legal and policy information easier to access, analyse, and use.

My approach is practical: understand the problem clearly, organise the evidence, and build tools or insights that support better decisions.
