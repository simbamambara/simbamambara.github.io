About

I am an applied economist, data scientist, and AI researcher working at the intersection of economics, law, public policy, and technology. My work focuses on using data and AI to solve practical problems in African institutions, including trade policy, taxation, consumer welfare, education, legal research, and decision-support systems. I am especially interested in how machine learning, information retrieval, and structured knowledge systems can make complex legal and policy information easier to access and use. I bring together economic analysis, research writing, statistical modelling, and software-based problem solving. My approach is practical: understand the problem clearly, organise the evidence, and build tools or insights that support better decisions.
